Design of an Electronic System for Queue Management at Hospitals

A patient-centred electronic token system designed to clarify hospital journeys, reduce waiting-time confusion, and improve communication among patients, attendants, and staff.

Project highlights

  • Conducted research across government and private hospital settings to map patient, attendant, and staff journeys and identify queue-management gaps.
  • Designed and prototyped an RFID-enabled token kiosk with haptic feedback, LCD display, and thermal-printing workflow for real-time patient interaction.
